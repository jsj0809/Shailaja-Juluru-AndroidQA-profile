### Title

App crashes by tapping on Sauce Labs Bike Light prodcut
 
### Prerequisites  

MYDEMOAPP has been successfully installed.

2. On product catalog screen, tap on “Sauce Labs Bike Light” product

Android OS 16.0

Mobile Phone : Samsung Galaxy S22  
App version: V.2.2.0-build 25
