### Title

Cart screen opens by tapping on a specific product  
 
### Prerequisites  

MYDEMOAPP has been successfully installed.  

### Steps To Reproduce  
1. Launch the app    
2. Tap on the first product available on the left side  
3. Add the product to cart by tapping on “Add to cart”      
4. Go back and tap on the first available product on the right side  

### Actual results    
Cart screen opens when tapped on the first available product on the right side.    

### Expected Results    
Product details page should open when tapped on the first available product on the right side.    

### Environment  
Android OS 16.0  
One UI version: 8.0  
Mobile Phone : Samsung S22  
App version: V.2.2.0-build 25  
### Severity  
Medium
