### Title

App crashes by tapping on Sauce Labs Bike Light prodcut
 
### Prerequisites  

MYDEMOAPP has been successfully installed.  

### Steps To Reproduce  
1. Launch the app    
2. On product catalog screen, tap on “Sauce Labs Bike Light” product
   
### Actual results    
App crashes by tapping on “Sauce Labs Bike Light” Product. 

### Expected Results    
Product details screen opens by tapping “Sauce Labs Bike Light” product.

### Environment  
Android OS 16.0  
One UI version: 8.0  
Mobile Phone : Samsung Galaxy S22  
App version: V.2.2.0-build 25  

### Severity  
Critical
